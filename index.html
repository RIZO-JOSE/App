01 · Problem & Solution

Problem & solution

😟

The problem

Lost items on campus rely on word of mouth, scattered posts and chance. Owners rarely learn where their things went, and finders have no trusted way to return them.

💡

The solution

One app where students report lost or found items with a photo, admins verify each post, and the finder and owner confirm the return together in chat.

02 · Key Features

Key features

📸

Report with photo

Lost or Found form with category, location, date and one photo from camera or gallery.

🔎

Search & filter

Lost / Found tabs, search bar and category chips on the Home dashboard.

💬

Chat & Mark as Done

Owner and finder chat with photo or video proof. Both sides must tap Mark as Done.

🛡️

Admin review

Every post waits for approval. Admins see Review, Claimed Items and support messages.

⭐

Honesty badge

Finders earn a star after a completed return, shown on their profile.

🔔

Alerts & support

Notifications with an unread filter, plus a support chat with the admin team.
